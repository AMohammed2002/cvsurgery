Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026

The short version. CardiacSchool runs entirely in your browser. Your progress, scores and settings are kept on your own device and never uploaded. There are no accounts, no cookies and no analytics. The only thing that leaves your device is a message you choose to send through the feedback form.

Who runs CardiacSchool

CardiacSchool is a small, independent study tool for medical students and residents learning adult cardiac surgery. It is run by an individual, not a company or institution. If you have a question about this policy or about your data, use the feedback form.

What is stored, and where

Everything the site remembers about you lives in your browser's own storage for this site (localStorage, sessionStorage and the browser cache). None of it is sent to us. If you open the site in a different browser or on a different device, it starts fresh, because there is nothing on our side to sync from.

All keys are prefixed cs_ so you can recognise them if you look in your browser's developer tools.

What leaves your device

Nothing, with one exception: the feedback form. When you submit it, your browser sends the form directly to Formspree (formspree.io), a third-party form service we use because the site has no server of its own. We then receive your message by email.

What the form sends:

  • the category you picked (for example, content error or bug);
  • the page you were on, so we can find what you are describing;
  • your message;
  • your email address, only if you choose to give one so we can reply.

Formspree also records the IP address and browser details of the submission as part of running its service. That is governed by Formspree's privacy policy, not ours. We keep feedback for as long as it takes to act on it and then delete it. Please do not put patient details or anything else confidential in the form.

Like every website, the host that serves our files sees the ordinary request data (your IP address and browser type) when your browser downloads a page. We do not use this to identify or follow you, and we run nothing that tracks you across pages or sites.

No cookies, no analytics

The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or social-media scripts. Fonts are served from our own files rather than a third-party font service. There is no account system, so we never hold your name, password or institution.

If analytics is added later. We may one day want to know roughly how many people use the site and which lessons are hardest. If we add that, it will either be cookieless and aggregate (no identifiers, no cross-site tracking) or it will ask for your consent first, and this page will be updated before it goes live.

Backing up, exporting and deleting your data

Because the data is on your device, you are in charge of it:

  • Export. On the Progress page you can download a JSON backup of everything listed above, and import it on another device.
  • Delete. On the same page, "Reset all progress" wipes the course data. To remove everything, including preferences and the offline cache, clear this site's data in your browser settings (usually under Privacy, Site data or Storage).

Once cleared, it is gone. We hold no copy and cannot restore it, so export first if you want to keep it.

Children

CardiacSchool is written for medical students, residents and qualified clinicians. It is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect almost nothing from anyone, the practical risk is small, but if you believe a child has sent us personal details through the feedback form, email us and we will delete them.

Changes to this policy

If the way the site handles data changes, for example if analytics or an optional sign-in is added, we will update this page and move the effective date at the top. Changes that matter will also be mentioned in the app the next time you open it.

Contact

Questions, corrections or a request to delete feedback you sent: use the feedback form.
