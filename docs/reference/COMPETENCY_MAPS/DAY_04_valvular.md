## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Place the operative moment on the natural-history curve for a named patient** and defend it
   against "wait until symptoms" — using the current triggers (asymptomatic severe AS with LVEF <50%,
   or very high gradient, or BNP >3× as a IIa nudge; primary MR with LVEF ≤60% or LVESD ≥40 mm).
2. **Reconcile COAPT vs MITRA-FR** via the proportionate/disproportionate framework (COAPT
   disproportionate MR, mean EROA ~0.41 cm², smaller LV, positive; MITRA-FR proportionate, ~0.31 cm²,
   dilated LV, neutral) and apply it to referring *this* secondary-MR patient.
3. **Resolve a discordant echo** (small AVA but low gradient) to a management decision with the next
   confirmatory test (dobutamine echo separating true-severe from pseudo-severe; CT calcium score),
   not a reflex threshold.
   → hinges on trials [COAPT/MITRA-FR, source [CO/MF]] and numbers [AS/MR severity thresholds].
