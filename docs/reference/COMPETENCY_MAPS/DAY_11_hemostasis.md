## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Run the bleeding chest as an evolving scenario** — trend drainage, ACT, temperature, fibrinogen,
   and TEG against the hemodynamics and decide at each step whether targeted correction is still
   appropriate or delay has become lethal (the surgical-vs-coagulopathic decision with rate-based
   re-exploration thresholds, and the "sudden stop then tamponade" trap).
2. **Manage the catastrophic Type III protamine reaction as an ordered algorithm** — stop protamine;
   support the circulation; if arriving at RV collapse, re-heparinize and return to bypass to unload
   the RV; treat pulmonary hypertension with an inhaled pulmonary vasodilator; protect the failing RV.
   → hinges on numbers [heparin ~300–400 u/kg to ACT >400–480; protamine ~1 mg/100 u; re-exploration
   >400 mL/hr ×1 h, >300 ×2–3 h, >200 ×4 h] and evidence anchors [viscoelastic-guided transfusion].
