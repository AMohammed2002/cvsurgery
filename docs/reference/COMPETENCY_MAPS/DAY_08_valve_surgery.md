## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Run a valve conference of hard calls and defend each on evidence + durability + patient values** —
   young bicuspid AS with a dilated aorta needing concomitant aortic replacement; small annulus forcing
   root-enlargement-vs-accept-mismatch; ischemic secondary MR where repair durability is poor and
   replacement may be the honest choice; the dialysis / pregnancy-considering patient; asymptomatic
   severe primary MR with repairable anatomy where early repair beats watchful waiting.
2. **Manage intraoperative SAM after mitral repair** — recognition on TEE, medical temporizing (volume,
   afterload up, stop inotropes, slow the rate), and surgical correction (sliding leaflet plasty or a
   larger ring to move coaptation posteriorly) with the decision to go back on pump.
   → hinges on trials [PARTNER 3 [P3], Evolut Low Risk [ELR]] and numbers [PPM burden device-dependent
   ~6.5% vs ~20–27%; indexed EOA <0.85 moderate / <0.65 severe mismatch; Carpentier classes].

**Viva bar:** *"Fit 68-year-old, severe symptomatic AS, 21 mm annulus — TAVR or SAVR, how do you avoid
patient-prosthesis mismatch, and if you open and the annulus is smaller than you sized for, what
exactly do you do?"*
