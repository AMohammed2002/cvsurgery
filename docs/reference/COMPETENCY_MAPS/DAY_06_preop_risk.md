## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **The genuinely hard triage calls where a low score misleads** — the frail octogenarian with severe
   AS and a porcelain aorta (low STS is misleading because it does not capture that cross-clamping a
   porcelain aorta is hazardous → transfemoral TAVR after Heart-Team review, not SAVR); the low-STS
   patient whose hostile chest, cirrhosis, or prior radiation the score does not capture.
2. **Shared-decision framing of mechanical vs tissue valve** in a 55-year-old who does not want
   lifelong anticoagulation — the durability-vs-anticoagulation tradeoff and the shrinking age
   threshold — and knowing when the right operation is *no* operation.
   → hinges on numbers [STS-PROM %; EuroSCORE II calibration; frailty markers — gait speed, albumin]
   and evidence anchors [STS vs EuroSCORE II calibration and shared blind spots].

**Viva bar:** *"Your STS says 1.8% but you are worried — name the three things the score is blind to in
this patient and show me how each one changes your recommendation."*

## Ladder coverage
- [ ] **L1–L3** — what "operative risk" means; STS-PROM vs EuroSCORE II (what each includes, EuroSCORE II better calibrated than the over-predicting logistic EuroSCORE I; STS as % 30-day mortality + morbidity); the shared blind spots (frailty, porcelain aorta, hostile chest, prior radiation, cirrhosis); optimization steps that change outcomes (anemia/iron, glycemic control, smoking cessation, prehabilitation, DAPT-interruption after a recent stent, carotid work-up, dental clearance before valve surgery); the pre-pump workup complete before the day of surgery.
- [ ] **L4** — building the case for operation (indication + risk two ways + modifiable factors + the one-paragraph "why now"); the frailty/futility assessment (gait speed, albumin, independence) that moves a borderline patient toward transcatheter therapy or palliation; the antithrombotic reconciliation for the specific stent/age/indication.
- [ ] **L5** — the two objectives above as when-not-to + evidence items.
