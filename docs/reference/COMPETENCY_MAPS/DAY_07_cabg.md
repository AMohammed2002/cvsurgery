## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Defend CABG vs PCI for a named anatomy/comorbidity using the trials at design level** — FREEDOM's
   diabetic-specific benefit (fewer death/MI at 5 y at the cost of more early stroke; limited by
   pre-current-DES enrollment); the EXCEL-vs-NOBLE left-main controversy (EXCEL non-inferior with a
   CK-MB-based periprocedural-MI definition that inflated the surgical count and drew a formal
   complaint; NOBLE favored CABG, primary composite ~28% PCI vs ~19% CABG; both industry-sponsored);
   the ART crossover problem (no BIMA survival benefit by ITT, HR ≈0.96, ~40% crossover, 22% radial);
   the RADIAL pooled analysis favoring radial over vein with the competitive-flow caveat.
2. **Read a coronary map and design + narrate the operation** — target quality/runoff,
   conduit-to-target matching, competitive-flow avoidance, sequential vs individual grafts, inflow
   strategy, anastomotic geometry — and manage the intraoperative surprises (porcelain aorta forcing
   anaortic/off-pump or alternative inflow; poor target at arteriotomy; new territory-specific
   wall-motion abnormality after separation) with their rescues.
   → hinges on trials [FREEDOM [FRE], EXCEL/NOBLE [EXC/NOB], ART [ART], RADIAL [RAD], SYNTAX] and
   numbers [LIMA-LAD >90% at 10 y; graft every vessel >1.5 mm; ACT >400 s; transit-time flow / PI].

**Viva bar:** *"Diabetic, three-vessel, SYNTAX 29, EF 40% — argue CABG over PCI with the trials at
design level, then give me your graft plan and tell me exactly what you do if the ascending aorta is a
rock when you go to clamp."*
