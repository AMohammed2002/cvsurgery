## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Escalate a failing heart one rung at a time** (optimize preload/afterload/rhythm → inotropes →
   IABP → temporary VAD or VA-ECMO → durable device or transplant) with the *specific trigger* for each
   step and the matching of device to physiology (isolated LV vs RV vs biventricular vs respiratory).
2. **The VA-ECMO patient whose LV has stopped ejecting with worsening pulmonary edema** — recognize LV
   distension (rising PA pressures, dilating LV on echo, pink froth) and choose a venting approach
   (Impella "ECpella," atrial septostomy, surgical LV vent, or IABP to reduce afterload); plus
   post-LVAD RV failure recognition/management (inotropic RV support, pulmonary vasodilators, avoid
   over-speeding the LVAD).
   → hinges on trials [IABP-SHOCK II [IABP]] and numbers [IABP net CO gain ~0.5 L/min; INTERMACS
   profiles; VA-ECMO afterload physiology].

**Viva bar:** *"Post-cardiotomy shock, on peripheral VA-ECMO, now the LV is not ejecting and the lungs
are whiting out — walk me through what is happening mechanistically and give me your venting options in
order of what you would reach for first."*

- [ ] **L1–L3** — IABP mechanism (diastolic augmentation raises coronary perfusion pressure; systolic deflation drops afterload/demand; net ~0.5 L/min), timing-error waveforms, contraindications (significant AR, dissection, severe PAD), and the honest evidence — IABP-SHOCK II showed no 30-day or 6-year mortality benefit (~66–67% mortality both arms) → downgraded routine IABP; VA vs VV ECMO physiology; durable continuous-flow LVAD physiology, INTERMACS profiles, complication set (RV failure post-implant, driveline infection, GI bleeding from acquired vWD/AVMs, pump thrombosis, suction events).
