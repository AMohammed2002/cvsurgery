# DAY 14 — Integration & PGY-5 Certification — COMPETENCY MAP & AUTHORING CHECKLIST

**Lessons:** none (assessment day). **Owned by Sequence 5.**
**Coupled assets:** the four faithful integration cases; the viva circuit; image/calculations/
operation-narration stations; the re-weighted written capstone; multi-dimensional readiness reporting.
**Depth floor (validate-depth.js):** ≥ 15 questions tagged `level >= 4` (re-weighted toward the L4/L5
`kind`s: operative-step, complication-rescue, when-not-to, dosed-algorithm, reconcile-discordant).

## Purpose
Prove the climb actually happened. A single 75-question recall exam cannot certify PGY-5 reasoning, so
Day 14 is a multi-format assessment, and the rebuild ends with a depth audit a human reviewer signs.

## Deliverables (Sequence 5 tasks)
1. **The four faithful integration cases** — reproduce the guide's four by their recognizable titles
   ("He Looks Fine But the Numbers Are Terrible," "She's Breathing Fast and Her Kidneys Are Going,"
   "The Rhythm Keeps Changing," "She Was Fine Yesterday") as branched interactive cases with graded
   choices (optimal / reasonable / suboptimal / harmful), consequences, and a debrief linking each
   error to the exact lesson/operation/lab/trial that repairs it.
2. **The viva circuit** — a rapid-fire defend-your-answer bank drawn from the per-day viva prompts
   (Sequences 2–4), escalating three levels deep (mechanism → evidence → exception).
3. **The stations** — imaging read-outs; the quantitative reconciliations (continuity equation, DO₂
   derivation, gradient/flow relationships); an operation-narration station (sequence + defend a case
   skin-to-skin).
4. **The written capstone** — keep the mock exam, re-weighted toward L4–L5 `kind`s over recall,
   drawing from the upgraded banks. Must carry ≥15 questions tagged `level >= 4`.
5. **Readiness reporting** — separate the score into knowledge, interpretation, operative sequencing,
   case management, crisis recognition, and retention.
6. **The final depth audit** — run `validate-depth.js` across all 14 days; clear `VERIFY_QUEUE.md`; and
   do a human medical-review pass sampling the L5 content of every day against the Part-1 yardstick.
