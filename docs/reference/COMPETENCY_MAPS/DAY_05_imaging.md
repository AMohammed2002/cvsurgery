## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Drive imaging to a decision under discordance** — the moderate-gradient / severe-AVA case, the
   next confirmatory test, and the honest limits of a static image.
2. **Recognize the intraoperative TEE finding that mandates going *back* on pump** — significant
   residual MR after repair, SAM with a meaningful LVOT gradient, a malseated or paravalvular-leaking
   prosthesis — and defend the re-pump decision to a team that wants to close.
   → hinges on numbers [continuity equation AVA and the squared LVOT-diameter error term; post-repair
   LVOT gradient] and evidence anchors [measurement-error catalog].

**Viva bar:** *"Post-mitral-repair TEE shows a 40 mmHg LVOT gradient and the anterior leaflet drifting
into the outflow tract in systole — what is it, what caused it, what do you try medically first, and
what do you do surgically before you leave the room?"*
