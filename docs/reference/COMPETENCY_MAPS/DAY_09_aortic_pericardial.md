## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Run the 2 a.m. type-A from ED call to sternotomy** — recognize it, image it (gated CT),
   resuscitate to permissive parameters *without delaying surgery*, choose the cannulation site for
   antegrade true-lumen flow, and manage the surprises (persistent malperfusion after a technically
   complete proximal repair, coronary-ostial involvement needing a button or graft, coagulopathic
   bleeding from friable tissue, a new post-op neurologic deficit).
2. **Defend the cannulation-site and arch-extent debate for the specific anatomy** — axillary/innominate
   or direct central true-lumen vs the retrograde-false-lumen/malperfusion risk of femoral return;
   hemiarch vs total arch vs frozen elephant trunk; root resuspension vs replacement.
   → hinges on numbers [ascending thresholds ~5.5 cm degenerative / ~5.0 or lower heritable / >0.5 cm/y;
   untreated type-A mortality ~1–2%/hr early] and evidence anchors [Stanford A vs B management].

**Viva bar:** *"Type A on CT with inferior ST elevation and a cold, pulseless right leg — what does each

pump?"*
