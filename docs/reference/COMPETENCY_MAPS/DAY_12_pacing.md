## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **The pacing-dependent, stiff-ventricle patient in whom losing AV synchrony collapses cardiac
   output** — why VVI is the *wrong* mode there (paces the ventricle but abandons the atrial kick the
   non-compliant hypertrophied LV depends on for filling) and DDD is right.
2. **The evidence-informed timing of permanent pacemaker implantation** and the conduction patterns
   that predict non-recovery (persistent complete block, new persistent LBBB with a long HV interval);
   plus the unstable rhythms (VT/VF, pause-dependent torsades, overdrive pacing) managed under a live
   hemodynamic trace.
   → hinges on numbers [post-op AF ~20–40%, peak POD 2–3; PPM observation window ~5–7 days; NBG modes].

**Viva bar:** *"POD 3 after AVR, complete heart block, currently paced VVI at 70 and the pressure is
80/50 with a rising lactate — tell me precisely what is wrong with the pacing, fix it at the box, and
tell me when and why you call EP for a permanent device."*

## Ladder coverage
- [ ] **L1–L3** — epicardial wire management (atrial vs ventricular, capture/sensing thresholds), NBG modes (AAI/VVI/DDD/DOO) and when each is used post-op, sensing/capture faults and their fixes; post-op AF (~20–40%, peak POD 2–3; beta-blockade/amiodarone prophylaxis, rate vs rhythm, the anticoagulation/stroke-risk decision if it persists).
