## L5 objectives (viva summits — write these FIRST, sourced)
1. **Run the unit for the deteriorating fresh post-op** — the localized posterior tamponade a TTE can
   miss and the decision to reopen the chest *at the bedside before arrest* rather than after, versus
   escalating to mechanical support when the pump itself is the problem.
2. **Distinguish vasoplegia from ventricular failure from tamponade from surgical bleeding at the
   bedside when the picture is mixed** — act on the leading diagnosis while hedging the others — and run
   the vasoplegia escalation ladder with the mechanism and dose of each rung.
   → hinges on numbers [LCOS CI <2.0–2.2; four Swan phenotypes; vasoplegia ladder doses —
   vasopressin ~0.03–0.04 u/min, methylene blue ~1.5–2 mg/kg, hydroxocobalamin ~5 g].

**Viva bar:** *"Fresh post-CABG, cardiac index 1.8, CVP and PA-diastolic rising and converging, chest
tubes that suddenly dropped from 200 to 20 mL/hr, and a falling pressure — what is your leading
diagnosis, what confirms it fastest, and what do you do in the next five minutes?"*

## Ladder coverage
- [ ] **L1–L3** — LCOS defined (CI <2.0–2.2 with rising lactate, oliguria, cool periphery); the four Swan phenotypes (hypovolemia low CVP/PCWP + low CI; pump failure high filling + low CI + low SvO₂; vasoplegia low SVR + high/normal CI + high SvO₂; tamponade equalized elevated diastolic pressures) and when a Swan changes management vs when it does not; the expected normal post-op trajectory.
- [ ] **L4** — the LCOS algorithm in order (preload → afterload → contractility → rate/rhythm → tamponade → ischemia → mechanical support) with drips titrated and dosed; the vasoplegia escalation ladder with the mechanism of each rung (norepinephrine → vasopressin → methylene blue → hydroxocobalamin → angiotensin II with the ATHOS-3 post-hoc caveat); RV-failure recognition/management; the other-organ complications (AKI/CRRT triggers, stroke/delirium, respiratory failure, sternal-wound infection/mediastinitis, mesenteric ischemia, post-pump SIRS).
- [ ] **L5** — the two objectives above as dosed-algorithm + reconcile-discordant + when-not-to + complication-rescue items.
